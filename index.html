About Me

I’m fascinated by what happens behind the scenes when raw data becomes something useful. With a background in Electronics and Communication Engineering and a Post Graduate Diploma in Business Analytics, I’m building my career around designing data pipelines that turn scattered information into reliable, analysis-ready data.

My toolkit includes Python, SQL, Azure Cloud, Databricks, Apache Airflow, and Docker. Through hands-on projects, I’ve explored ETL pipelines, real-time data streaming, cloud-based data platforms, and data visualization.

I enjoy solving data problems, automating repetitive workflows, and learning how to build systems that can handle data efficiently at scale. My goal is to develop reliable data infrastructure that helps analysts, data scientists, and businesses make better decisions.
